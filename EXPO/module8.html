SUSTAINABILITY

Principles of Sustainability

1. Intergenerational Equity
Ensures that current development does not harm future generations. Promotes conservation of resources like water, energy, and biodiversity.

2. Precautionary Principle
If an action may cause harm to the environment or human health, precautionary measures should be taken even if some cause-and-effect relationships are not fully established scientifically.

3. Polluter Pays Principle
Those who produce pollution should bear the cost of managing it to prevent damage to human health or the environment.

4. Conservation of Biodiversity
Protecting ecosystems and the variety of species to maintain balance and resilience in nature.

5. Efficiency in Resource Use
Promoting the use of renewable resources and reducing waste through recycling and sustainable practices.

6. Social Inclusiveness
Ensuring that all communities benefit from development, with attention to equity, education, and poverty reduction..

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) is a process used to evaluate the environmental effects of a proposed project or development before it begins.
It ensures that potential negative impacts on the environment are identified, assessed, and minimized.
